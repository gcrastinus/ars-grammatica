Ars Grammatica

The first road of the trivium
Grammar is the art of putting words together rightly. It is the first of the three roads because logic works upon statements, and a statement is made of words. This course assumes none of it. It builds from the voice to the sentence, gives the traditional names as they come, and asks at every step the question the medieval masters asked and we have mostly stopped asking: why are the parts of speech what they are, and not something else?
The Partner Language
English and Latin run through every part of this course. Latin is the tradition’s own language, and every technical term here is a Latin term. You may add one further witness.
