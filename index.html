Ars Grammatica

The first road of the trivium
Grammar is the art of putting words together rightly. It is the first of the three roads because logic works upon statements, and a statement is made of words. This course builds from the voice to the sentence, gives the traditional names in the appropriate places, and considers the more general question of why the parts of speech are the way they are.
The Partner Language
English and Latin run through every part of this course. Latin is the tradition’s own language, and every technical term here is a Latin term. You may add one further witness.

Quiz Maker

Printable quizzes with a complete answer key — for instructors
How to use

Select one or more exercise sets, choose the difficulty and how many questions you want from each, then generate. Questions are freshly drawn from the same generators the students use — including those that take from a finite corpus of authors. The answer key prints on its own page(s) at the end. Separate those pages before handing the quiz out. Use the browser’s Print → Save as PDF.

The quiz is of the exercises. You may also include two (or more) major doctrine questions from the study tutorials that belong to the sets you chose. Open questions left for later study are not used.
