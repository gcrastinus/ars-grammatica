# Greek notes needed

This list was made in the step that added Greek as a partner language. No Greek notes were added to the course in that step. Line numbers are those of index.html at the commit "Greek: one-blank exercise".

## 1. Existing text that speaks of Latin where a Greek student would need a note

The list comes from a search of the deck panels, the exercise intros and descriptions, and the item banks of the exercises that could later offer Greek, for these subjects: the ablative; the article where Latin is named; the number of cases; declensions and conjugations; voice (active and passive, deponents), where Greek has a middle; the perfect and the aorist; the subjunctive and the optative; the dual; the supine and the gerundive; the Latin accent rule (penult, antepenult, macrons); and statements of the form "Latin has no …" or "Latin does not have …". It leaves out the English-only banks, the Latin-only exercises (latnoun, latverb, latbuild, latmore, latvoice, latclause, latsubj, period, servius4, livyperiod, auctores), the four other arts in section IX, and the dormant third-language items listed in part 2. A search by subject can miss a card that makes a Latin claim in other words, so the list is a starting point for a reading of the course, not a complete one. Answer options and sentences that already speak of Greek are left out.
